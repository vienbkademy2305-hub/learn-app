Bài 4 · True / False / Not Given

Đọc đoạn văn rồi chọn T (đúng), F (sai) hoặc NG (không được nhắc đến).

Vietnam has very different weather in the north and the south. In the north, there are four seasons. Winter is cold and humid, and in the mountains it is sometimes freezing. Summer is hot, with a lot of storms. In the south, there are only two seasons: the dry season and the rainy season. The temperature is usually between 25 and 35 degrees. Mai lives in Can Tho and loves the dry season. Her cousin Hung lives in Hanoi. He is afraid of the cold, so he doesn't like winter.

  1. 1. The south of Vietnam has four seasons.

  2. 2. It is sometimes freezing in the northern mountains.

  3. 3. Mai doesn't like the dry season.

  4. 4. Hung likes winter.

  5. 5. Mai is afraid of storms.

  6. 6. Hung visits Can Tho every year.

Nghe hội thoại (mỗi người một giọng) rồi chọn đáp án đúng. Cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu, chưa được thì nghe lại.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  2. 2. What is the weather like in Ho Chi Minh City?

  3. 3. What does the forecast say?

Bài 9 · Đúng / Sai (True / False)

Nghe rồi chọn T (đúng) hoặc F (sai). Chú ý chủ thể, con số và các từ như always / never / only.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  2. 2. It takes about seven hours by bus from Ho Chi Minh City.

  3. 3. Many tourists are interested in the flower gardens.

  4. 4. Nights in winter are warm.
