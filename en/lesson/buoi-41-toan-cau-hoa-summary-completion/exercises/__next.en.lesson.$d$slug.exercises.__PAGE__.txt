3:T5fd,THE JOURNEY OF A SMARTPHONE

A  A modern smartphone is a truly global product. It may be designed in California, but its parts come from dozens of countries. The screen might be made in South Korea, the memory chips in Taiwan, and the battery in China. Finally, all the parts are shipped to a factory, often in Vietnam or India, where the phone is put together.

B  This system, known as a global supply chain, allows companies to keep prices low. Each country does what it can do most cheaply or most skilfully, and consumers around the world benefit from cheaper and better products. For developing countries, joining these supply chains has brought millions of factory jobs.

C  However, the system is also fragile. In 2021, a shortage of computer chips stopped production in car and phone factories on three continents. Because every step depends on the one before, a problem in a single factory can delay products all over the world. Since then, some multinational corporations have started moving part of their production closer to home, even though this increases costs.
