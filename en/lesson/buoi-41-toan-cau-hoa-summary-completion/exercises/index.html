Bài 1 · Điền thông tin

Summary KHÔNG có hộp từ: đọc bài và hoàn thành đoạn tóm tắt. Viết KHÔNG QUÁ HAI TỪ lấy từ bài đọc.

THE JOURNEY OF A SMARTPHONE A A modern smartphone is a truly global product. It may be designed in California, but its parts come from dozens of countries. The screen might be made in South Korea, the memory chips in Taiwan, and the battery in China. Finally, all the parts are shipped to a factory, often in Vietnam or India, where the phone is put together. B This system, known as a global supply chain, allows companies to keep prices low. Each country does what it can do most cheaply or most skilfully, and consumers around the world benefit from cheaper and better products. For developing countries, joining these supply chains has brought millions of factory jobs. C However, the system is also fragile. In 2021, a shortage of computer chips stopped production in car and phone factories on three continents. Because every step depends on the one before, a problem in a single factory can delay products all over the world. Since then, some multinational corporations have started moving part of their production closer to home, even though this increases costs. SUMMARY A smartphone's parts are made in many countries and finally (1) ___ in a factory, often in Vietnam or India. This global supply chain keeps (2) ___ low, and consumers get better products. It has also created millions of (3) ___ in developing countries. But the system can easily break: in 2021, a lack of (4) ___ stopped production on three continents. As a result, some companies are moving production closer to home, despite the higher (5) ___.

Tối đa 2 từ mỗi chỗ trống.

  1. 1. (1)
  2. 2. (2)
  3. 3. (3)
  4. 4. (4)
  5. 5. (5)

Bài 2 · Điền thông tin

Summary CÓ hộp từ: chọn một từ trong hộp cho mỗi chỗ trống (viết nguyên từ). Có 3 từ thừa. Đáp án thường là từ ĐỒNG NGHĨA với từ trong bài.

Passage: "In many Asian cities, global fast-food chains have become extremely popular with teenagers. Some sociologists fear that traditional family meals are disappearing, as young people prefer to eat quickly and cheaply outside the home. Others point out that local restaurants have also changed, adding modern dishes to attract younger customers, so local food culture is adapting rather than dying." SUMMARY International fast-food restaurants are very (1) ___ among young people in Asia. Some experts are (2) ___ that families no longer eat traditional meals together. However, local restaurants have (3) ___ new dishes to their menus, which shows that food culture is changing, not (4) ___. BOX: popular · worried · added · disappearing · removed · happy · unpopular

Tối đa 1 từ mỗi chỗ trống.

  1. 1. (1)
  2. 2. (2)
  3. 3. (3)
  4. 4. (4)

Bài 3 · Chia động từ

Chia động từ trong ngoặc: V-ing hay to V.

  1. 1. (travel) abroad has become cheaper for young Vietnamese.
  2. 2. Many consumers are interested in (buy) local products.
  3. 3. It is difficult for small shops (compete) with global brands.
  4. 4. The company avoided (raise) its prices.
  5. 5. Most families can't afford (buy) imported cars.
  6. 6. You can learn about other cultures by (watch) foreign films.
  7. 7. The manager suggested (move) production to Vietnam.
  8. 8. The factory refused (pay) higher wages.
