7 bài. Máy chấm không phân biệt hoa thường và dạng viết tắt (don't = do not), nhưng chấm chặt chính tả.

Bài 1 · Chia động từ

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở dạng bị động (hiện tại đơn hoặc quá khứ đơn).

  1. 1. Tea (grow) in Thai Nguyen.
  2. 2. This temple (build) in the eleventh century.
  3. 3. These shoes (make) in Vietnam.
  4. 4. The first computer (invent) in the 1940s.
  5. 5. Rice (export) to many countries every year.
  6. 6. The letters (send) yesterday.
  7. 7. English (speak) in many countries.
  8. 8. The windows (not / clean) last week.

Bài 2 · Biến đổi câu

Viết lại câu ở dạng bị động. Chỉ giữ by + người khi cần thiết.

  1. 1. Farmers grow coffee in the Central Highlands. → Coffee
  2. 2. They built this school in 1995. → This school
  3. 3. Picasso painted this picture. → This picture
  4. 4. People speak French in Canada. → French
  5. 5. The factory produces 500 bikes a day. → 500 bikes

Bài 3 · Trắc nghiệm

Chọn đáp án đúng.

  1. 1. This table is made ___ wood.

  2. 2. My phone was made ___ Korea.

  3. 3. The book ___ into ten languages last year.

  4. 4. Where ___ you born?

  5. 5. This song was written ___ a Vietnamese singer.

Bài 4 · Nối từ – định nghĩa

Nối từ với định nghĩa tiếng Anh.

  1. export
  2. invention
  3. handmade
  4. recycle
  5. factory

Bài 5 · True / False / Not Given

Đọc đoạn văn rồi chọn T (đúng), F (sai) hoặc NG (không được nhắc đến).

Van Phuc is a small village near Hanoi. It is famous for its silk. In the past, all the silk was made here by hand, but today most of the cloth is produced by machines. The village shops sell scarves, shirts and dresses to tourists. Some of the products are exported to Japan and France. The oldest workshop in the village was opened by a local family more than eighty years ago, and it is still run by the same family today.

  1. 1. Van Phuc is famous for pottery.

  2. 2. Most silk in the village is made by hand today.

  3. 3. Some silk products are sold to other countries.

  4. 4. Silk from Van Phuc is more expensive than silk from Hoi An.

  5. 5. The oldest workshop was opened by a family from another city.

  6. 6. The same family still runs the oldest workshop.

Bài 6 · Trắc nghiệm

Nghe hội thoại (mỗi người một giọng) rồi chọn đáp án đúng. Cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu, chưa được thì nghe lại.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  1. 1. Where was the scarf made?

  2. 2. What is it made of?

  3. 3. Where are some scarves exported to?

Bài 7 · Đúng / Sai (True / False)

Nghe rồi chọn T (đúng) hoặc F (sai). Chú ý chủ thể, con số và các từ như always / never / only.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  1. 1. Coffee is grown in the Central Highlands.

  2. 2. Coffee was brought to Vietnam by the Chinese.

  3. 3. Vietnamese coffee is exported to more than eighty countries.

  4. 4. Vietnamese coffee is never used for instant coffee.
