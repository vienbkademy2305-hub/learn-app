Bài 5 · True / False / Not Given

Đọc đoạn văn rồi chọn T (đúng), F (sai) hoặc NG (không được nhắc đến).

Hoa and her brother Tuan have very different hobbies. Hoa loves being outside. Every Sunday she goes cycling with her club, and in the summer she often goes camping. Tuan prefers staying at home. He enjoys playing chess online and he is learning to play the piano. Last year Tuan decided to join a music club, but he stopped going after two months because it was too far from his house. Their mother wants them to spend more time together, so they now play board games every Friday evening.

  1. 1. Hoa goes cycling every weekend.

  2. 2. Tuan still goes to the music club.

  3. 3. The music club was near Tuan's house.

  4. 4. Tuan plays the piano better than Hoa.

  5. 5. Hoa likes camping.

  6. 6. Their father wants them to spend more time together.
