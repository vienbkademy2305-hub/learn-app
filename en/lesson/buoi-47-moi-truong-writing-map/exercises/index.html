Bài 1 · Điền từ

demolishedconvertedextendedrelocatedreplacedconstructed

Bài 2 · Trắc nghiệm

Chọn đáp án đúng.

  1. 1. Maps: 2000 and now. 'Since 2000, a hospital ___ in the north.'

  2. 2. Maps: 1980 and 2010. 'In 2010, the farm ___ by houses.'

  3. 3. The woodland ___ a car park.

  4. 4. The church in the centre ___.

  5. 5. Which sentence is best for Task 1 maps?

Bài 3 · Biến đổi câu

Viết lại câu dùng từ trong ngoặc, giữ nguyên nghĩa.

  1. 1. They knocked down the old houses and built a car park there. (replaced by)
  2. 2. The warehouse became a hotel. (converted into)
  3. 3. The trees were removed and a road was built. (made way for)
  4. 4. Nobody changed the school. (remained unchanged)

Bài 5 · Điền thông tin

Hoàn thành bài map (bản đồ mô tả bằng lời). Viết MỘT TỪ cho mỗi chỗ trống.

2005: a school with a sports field in the east; a car park in the west; trees in the south. Now: sports field → new science building; car park expanded; trees removed → bus stop; main building unchanged. The maps show how a school has changed since 2005. Overall, the school has become more developed, although the main building has (1) ___ unchanged. In the east, the sports field has been (2) ___ by a new science building. The car park in the west has been (3) ___, and the trees in the south have been (4) ___ to make (5) ___ for a bus stop.

Tối đa 1 từ mỗi chỗ trống.

  1. 1. (1)
  2. 2. (2)
  3. 3. (3)
  4. 4. (4)
  5. 5. (5)

Bài 7 · Trắc nghiệm

Nghe hai người nói về kế hoạch cải tạo công viên (hai giọng) và chọn chữ cái đúng (A–F) cho mỗi địa điểm MỚI. Có 2 chữ cái thừa.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

Park plan (main gate in the south): A – inside the park, just north of the main gate B – outside the park, east of the main gate C – beside the lake, in the centre D – north-west corner E – north-east corner F – along the west side of the park

  1. 1. new playground

  2. 2. new car park

  3. 3. café (new position)

  4. 4. cycle lane
