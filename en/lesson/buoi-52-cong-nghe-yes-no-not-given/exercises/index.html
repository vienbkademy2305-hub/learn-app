Bài 1 · Yes / No / Not Given

Đọc bài (khoảng 8 phút). Các nhận định có khớp với QUAN ĐIỂM của tác giả không? Chọn Y (Yes), N (No) hoặc NG (Not Given). Tách ý người khác và ý tác giả trước khi chọn.

SHOULD WE FEAR ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE? Few technologies have been discussed as much as artificial intelligence. Some commentators warn that intelligent machines will soon take over most jobs and even threaten human survival. Others promise that AI will cure diseases and end poverty within a generation. In my view, both groups exaggerate. There is no doubt that machine learning has produced genuine breakthroughs. Systems that analyse medical scans are, in some studies, as accurate as experienced doctors, and translation software has improved at an unprecedented speed. However, these systems are narrow: a program that identifies skin cancer cannot drive a car or write a poem. The idea that one machine will soon match human intelligence in every area is, I believe, not supported by the evidence. That does not mean we should relax. The more immediate danger is not a super-intelligent robot but ordinary algorithms that make decisions about people's lives without anyone checking them. Algorithms already help decide who gets a loan, who is invited to a job interview and, in some countries, who receives police attention. If the data they learn from reflects past discrimination, they are likely to repeat it — and because their decisions look scientific, they may be harder to challenge than a biased human. The answer, I would argue, is not to ban AI but to regulate it sensibly. Companies should be required to explain how important decisions are made, and a human should always be able to review them. Such rules may slow innovation slightly, but that is a small price for public trust.

  1. 1. The writer agrees that AI will take over most jobs soon.

  2. 2. The writer accepts that machine learning has achieved real progress.

  3. 3. The writer believes medical AI is more accurate than all doctors.

  4. 4. The writer thinks translation software will soon replace human translators.

  5. 5. According to the writer, ordinary algorithms are a more urgent risk than super-intelligent machines.

  6. 6. The writer suggests that biased algorithms may be harder to challenge than biased people.

  7. 7. The writer believes AI should be banned in the police.

  8. 8. The writer thinks new rules would greatly reduce companies' profits.

Bài 2 · Trắc nghiệm

Chọn đáp án đúng về mức độ chắc chắn.

  1. 1. AI ___ to change most office jobs in the next ten years.

  2. 2. Which sentence is the most certain?

  3. 3. Which sentence is the least certain?

  4. 4. Passage: 'AI may help teachers mark essays.' Statement: 'The writer believes AI will replace teachers.' →

  5. 5. ___, privacy is the biggest risk of facial recognition.

Bài 3 · Biến đổi câu

Viết lại câu với từ trong ngoặc, giữ nguyên mức độ.

  1. 1. AI will probably change education. (likely) → AI
  2. 2. It is possible that autonomous cars will reduce accidents. (may) → Autonomous cars
  3. 3. Robots probably won't replace nurses soon. (unlikely) → Robots
  4. 4. The algorithm seems to prefer men. (appears) → The algorithm

Bài 7 · Trắc nghiệm

Nghe hai người thảo luận (hai giọng) và chọn đáp án đúng. Chú ý mức độ chắc chắn.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  1. 1. What does the council claim?

  2. 2. What does the woman think about the council's claim?

  3. 3. What is the woman's main worry?

  4. 4. Under what condition might she accept the cameras?
