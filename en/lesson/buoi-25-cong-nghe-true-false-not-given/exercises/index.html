Bài 1 · True / False / Not Given

Đọc bài và chọn T (TRUE), F (FALSE) hoặc NG (NOT GIVEN). Nhớ: không thấy thông tin → NG, không phải F. Kiểm tra chủ thể trước khi chọn.

Robots in Vietnamese factories In the past ten years, many factories in Vietnam have started to use robots. The first large group of industrial robots arrived at a car factory in Hai Phong in 2017. Today, robots which weld metal parts can work 24 hours a day without a break, and they make fewer mistakes than human workers. However, automation has not replaced all factory jobs. Most clothing factories still need thousands of workers, because robots cannot yet sew soft materials as well as people. In electronics factories, on the other hand, robots now do most of the work on the production line. Some workers who lost their jobs to robots have joined training courses. A programme in Binh Duong, which started in 2021, teaches workers how to repair and control robots. So far, about 3,000 people have finished the course, and most of them have found new jobs in the same factories.

  1. 1. Vietnamese factories have used robots for more than twenty years.

  2. 2. The first big group of industrial robots went to a car factory.

  3. 3. Welding robots make more mistakes than people.

  4. 4. Robots are cheaper than workers in clothing factories.

  5. 5. In clothing factories, robots do most of the work.

  6. 6. The training programme in Binh Duong started in 2021.

  7. 7. The training course is free for workers.

  8. 8. Most people who finished the course found new jobs.

Bài 2 · Yes / No / Not Given

Bài viết thể hiện QUAN ĐIỂM của tác giả. Chọn Y (YES — đúng quan điểm tác giả), N (NO — trái quan điểm tác giả) hoặc NG (NOT GIVEN — không biết tác giả nghĩ gì).

Many parents believe that smartphones are bad for children. In my view, the problem is not the phone itself but how long children use it. A phone can be a useful learning tool: my students use dictionary apps and watch science videos that make lessons more interesting. However, I believe that children under ten should not have their own phones, because they cannot control their screen time. Schools also have a role to play. Instead of banning phones completely, schools should teach digital literacy, so that students learn to find reliable information and avoid fake news.

  1. 1. Smartphones are always harmful to children.

  2. 2. Phones can help children learn.

  3. 3. Children under ten should not own a phone.

  4. 4. Schools should ban phones completely.

  5. 5. Parents should use apps to check their children's messages.

Bài 3 · Nối câu

Nối hai câu thành một câu, dùng who, which hoặc where.

  1. 1. I have a friend. She works for a software company.
  2. 2. This is the robot. It cleans our office every night.
  3. 3. Users download this app. They can learn English for free.
  4. 4. That is the café. I usually work online there.
  5. 5. The laptop was expensive. I bought it last week.

Bài 7 · Trắc nghiệm

Nghe hội thoại (mỗi người một giọng) rồi chọn đáp án đúng. Cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu, chưa được thì nghe lại.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  1. 1. What does the app give the man every day?

  2. 2. Where is the company based?

  3. 3. What data does the app keep?

Bài 8 · Đúng / Sai (True / False)

Nghe rồi chọn T (đúng) hoặc F (sai). Chú ý chủ thể, con số và các từ như always / never / only.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  1. 1. The survey asked one thousand teenagers.

  2. 2. Most teenagers spend less than five hours a day on their phones.

  3. 3. Teenagers who use social media late at night often sleep well.

  4. 4. Most teenagers want to reduce their screen time.
