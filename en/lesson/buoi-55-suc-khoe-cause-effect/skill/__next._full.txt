1f:T40a,Question: In many countries, the number of children who are overweight is increasing. What are the causes of this? What effects does it have?

Body 1: There are several factors behind this trend. The most important is diet. Processed food and sugary drinks have become cheap and widely available, and they are heavily advertised to children; consequently, many young people consume far more sugar than they need. Another key factor contributing to obesity is a sedentary lifestyle. Owing to the popularity of smartphones and computer games, children now spend much less time playing outside than previous generations did.
