Bài 1 · Trắc nghiệm

Chọn đáp án đúng.

  1. 1. Housing (40%) was ___ higher than clothing (5%).

  2. 2. City A (30.1%) was ___ higher than City B (29.8%).

  3. 3. Food was ___ the largest category.

  4. 4. ___ income people have, the more they spend on leisure.

  5. 5. Spending on transport was almost ___ spending on food.

  6. 6. Online sales ___ 50% of the total in 2020.

Bài 2 · Biến đổi câu

Viết lại câu theo gợi ý, giữ nguyên nghĩa.

  1. 1. People earn more. They spend more. → The more
  2. 2. No other category was nearly as large as housing. → Housing was by far
  3. 3. Spending on food was 20% and spending on transport was 19%. → Spending on transport was marginally
  4. 4. The number of online shoppers became three times higher. → The number of online shoppers
