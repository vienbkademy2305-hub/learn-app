8 bài. Máy chấm không phân biệt hoa thường và dạng viết tắt (don't = do not), nhưng chấm chặt chính tả.

Bài 1 · Biến đổi câu

Ôn cấu trúc GĐ3: viết lại câu theo gợi ý.

  1. 1. Online shopping saves time. It also encourages impulse buying. → Not only
  2. 2. The city didn't fund the theatre. It closed. → If the city had
  3. 3. The cost worries taxpayers most. → What
  4. 4. She finished the report. Then she sent it to her manager. → Having
  5. 5. Many countries have banned animal testing. → Animal testing

Bài 2 · Trắc nghiệm

Chấm thử: mỗi nhận xét thuộc tiêu chí nào?

  1. 1. 'The essay does not answer the second question.'

  2. 2. 'Moreover is used in almost every sentence.'

  3. 3. 'There are frequent errors with plural nouns.'

  4. 4. 'Collocations such as "impose a ban on" are used accurately.'

  5. 5. 'The Task 1 report has no overview.'

  6. 6. 'Each paragraph has a clear central topic.'

Bài 4 · True / False / Not Given

Reading (mô phỏng, 6 phút): đọc đoạn văn và chọn T, F hoặc NG.

When the public band descriptors for IELTS Writing were revised, examiners were given clearer guidance on how to assess each criterion. According to the descriptors, a Task 1 report without an overview is unlikely to score above band 5 for task achievement, however accurate its figures are. In Task 2, candidates who fail to address every part of the question are also limited, although the exact penalty depends on how much of the task is missing. Interestingly, the descriptors do not reward long essays: an answer of 400 words is not marked higher than one of 270 words simply because of its length. Examiners are, however, told to note when an answer is under the minimum word count.

  1. 1. A Task 1 report with accurate figures but no overview can still reach band 6 for task achievement.

  2. 2. In Task 2, the penalty for missing part of the question is always the same.

  3. 3. Longer essays receive higher marks because of their length.

  4. 4. Examiners pay attention to answers that are too short.

  5. 5. Most candidates write more than 300 words in Task 2.
