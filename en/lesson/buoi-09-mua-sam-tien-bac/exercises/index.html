Ngữ pháp: So sánh nhất, not as … as (ôn so sánh hơn)

8 bài. Máy chấm không phân biệt hoa thường và dạng viết tắt (don't = do not), nhưng chấm chặt chính tả.

Bài 1 · Nối từ – định nghĩa

Nối từ với định nghĩa tiếng Anh.

  1. receipt
  2. discount
  3. bargain
  4. wallet
  5. afford

Bài 2 · Dạng từ

Viết dạng so sánh nhất của từ trong ngoặc.

  1. 1. This is shop in the city. (cheap)(cheap)
  2. 2. That's phone in the store. (expensive)(expensive)
  3. 3. Saturday is day at the market. (busy)(busy)
  4. 4. It's shopping centre in Vietnam. (big)(big)
  5. 5. This is price of the year! (good)(good)
  6. 6. It was day of my life. (bad)(bad)
  7. 7. This is brand in my country. (popular)(popular)

Bài 3 · Biến đổi câu

Viết lại câu dùng not as … as, giữ nguyên nghĩa.

  1. 1. The market is cheaper than the supermarket. → The supermarket
  2. 2. My bag is bigger than your bag. → Your bag
  3. 3. This jacket is warmer than that one. → That jacket
  4. 4. Credit cards are more convenient than cash. → Cash

Bài 4 · Trắc nghiệm

Chọn đáp án đúng.

  1. 1. It's the ___ market in town.

  2. 2. She is the tallest girl ___ her class.

  3. 3. This shirt isn't as nice ___ that one.

  4. 4. How much ___ these shoes cost?

  5. 5. Can I pay ___ credit card?

Bài 5 · True / False / Not Given

Đọc đoạn văn rồi chọn T (đúng), F (sai) hoặc NG (không được nhắc đến).

There are three places to shop in my town. The market is the oldest and the cheapest, but it is very crowded in the morning, and you can only pay in cash. The supermarket is not as cheap as the market, but it is cleaner and you can pay by card. The new shopping centre is the most expensive place. It has famous brands, a cinema and a lot of restaurants. My mother prefers the market, but my sister goes to the shopping centre every weekend.

  1. 1. The supermarket is cheaper than the market.

  2. 2. You can use a card at the supermarket.

  3. 3. The shopping centre is the newest place.

  4. 4. The shopping centre has a supermarket inside.

  5. 5. The writer's sister likes the market best.

  6. 6. The market is busy in the morning.

Bài 6 · Trắc nghiệm

Chọn đáp án đúng (trang phục + so sánh).

  1. 1. It's cold outside. Take your ___.

  2. 2. My new jeans ___ too long.

  3. 3. These are ___ trousers in the shop.

  4. 4. I bought ___ at the market yesterday.

  5. 5. This skirt is not ___ that dress.

  6. 6. For a party with friends, I wear ___ clothes: jeans and a T-shirt.

Bài 7 · Trắc nghiệm

Nghe hội thoại (mỗi người một giọng) rồi chọn đáp án đúng. Cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu, chưa được thì nghe lại.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  1. 1. How much is the jumper before the discount?

  2. 2. How big is the discount?

  3. 3. How can the man pay?

Bài 8 · Đúng / Sai (True / False)

Nghe rồi chọn T (đúng) hoặc F (sai). Chú ý chủ thể, con số và các từ như always / never / only.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  1. 1. Ben Thanh is one of the oldest markets in the city.

  2. 2. Ben Thanh is cheaper than small local markets.

  3. 3. You can bargain at Ben Thanh.

  4. 4. The shopping centre is the cheapest place.
