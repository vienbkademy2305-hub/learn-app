Bài 1 · Điền từ

Chọn cách diễn đạt phân số phù hợp với con số trong ngoặc.

nearly halfjust over a quartera thirdthree quartersa small minorityjust over half
  1. 1. Electronics made up of exports. (48%)
  2. 2. Clothes accounted for of the total. (26%)
  3. 3. Rice represented only of exports. (3%)
  4. 4. of the goods went to Asia. (75%)
  5. 5. The USA received of all exports. (33%)
  6. 6. of households owned a car. (53%)

  3. 3. 30% of the money ___ spent on food.

Bài 3 · Biến đổi câu

Viết lại câu với từ trong ngoặc, giữ nguyên nghĩa.

  1. 1. Electronics were 45% of exports. (accounted for)
  2. 2. Tourism was 12% of the total. (made up)
  3. 3. Food was 20% and housing was 35%. (respectively)
  4. 4. Oil and gas together were 18%. (Combined)
