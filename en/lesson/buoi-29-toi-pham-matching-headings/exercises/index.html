Bài 1 · Trắc nghiệm

Matching headings: chọn tiêu đề đúng cho mỗi đoạn. Có 2 tiêu đề thừa; mỗi tiêu đề chỉ dùng một lần.

Headings: i Punishments that keep offenders out of prison ii A fall in one type of crime iii Why prison does not always work iv Technology that helps the police v The history of prisons in Asia vi Crimes committed by young people online A Over the last decade, the number of house burglaries in many European cities has dropped. Experts say this is because more homes have strong locks and alarms, and because fewer people keep cash at home. B Many people believe that sending criminals to prison makes society safer. However, research shows that about half of all prisoners commit another crime within two years of leaving. In prison, young offenders often meet more experienced criminals and learn new ways to break the law. C For this reason, some countries are trying other punishments. Offenders may be ordered to do unpaid community work, such as cleaning parks, or they may have to wear an electronic tag that shows where they are. These punishments are much cheaper than prison. D Modern tools have also changed police work. Security cameras now cover most city centres, and computer programs can recognise faces or car number plates in seconds. In some cities, drones are being used to follow suspects.
