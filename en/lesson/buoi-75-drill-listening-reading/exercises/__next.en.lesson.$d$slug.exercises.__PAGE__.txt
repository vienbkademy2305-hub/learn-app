3:T433,TWO STUDIES ON HOMEWORK

Two research teams recently studied the effects of homework on secondary school students. The first, led by Dr Anna Kerr, followed 600 students in Scotland for three years. The second, led by Professor Ming Li, studied 1,200 students in Singapore for one year.

Kerr's team found that students who did about an hour of homework a night achieved better exam results than those who did none. However, students who did more than two hours a night did no better than the one-hour group, and they reported higher levels of stress. Li's study reached a similar conclusion about the benefits of moderate homework, but found no link between homework time and stress. The latter team suggested that this difference might be explained by the greater support that Singaporean students receive from tutors outside school.
