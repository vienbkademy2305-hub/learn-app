Bài 5 · True / False / Not Given

Đọc đoạn văn rồi chọn T (đúng), F (sai) hoặc NG (không được nhắc đến).

It is Monday morning at Hoa Binh High School. In class 10A, the students are having a geography lesson. Their teacher, Mr Long, is showing them a map of Vietnam. Most students are taking notes, but two boys at the back are talking. In class 10B, the students are not in their classroom. They are playing football because it is their PE lesson. Their teacher is watching them from the side of the field.

  1. 1. Class 10A is studying geography.

  2. 2. Mr Long is playing football with class 10B.

  3. 3. All the students in 10A are taking notes.

  4. 4. Mr Long is a new teacher at the school.

  5. 5. Class 10B is outside the classroom.

  6. 6. The students in 10B like PE more than geography.
