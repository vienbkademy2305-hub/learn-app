Bài 1 · Chia động từ

Chia động từ trong ngoặc (điều kiện loại 3 hoặc hỗn hợp).

  1. 1. If we (leave) earlier, we wouldn't have missed the start of the film.
  2. 2. If the critics had liked it, the play (run) for longer.
  3. 3. If I had learnt the guitar as a child, I (play) in a band now.
  4. 4. If she weren't so shy, she (audition) for the lead role last month.
  5. 5. The film might (win) an award if the ending had been better.

Bài 2 · Nối câu

Viết một câu điều kiện loại 3 hoặc hỗn hợp nói về điều ngược lại.

  1. 1. The tickets sold out. We didn't see the show.
  2. 2. The gallery didn't get funding. It closed.
  3. 3. I didn't study music. I'm not a musician now.
  4. 4. The sequel was boring. It didn't do well at the box office.

Bài 3 · Trắc nghiệm

Matching features: đọc bài và chọn người phù hợp với mỗi nhận định (A Moreno, B Chen, C Park). Một người có thể được chọn nhiều lần.

WHO SHOULD PAY FOR THE ARTS? Theatre critic Laura Moreno believes that public money is essential for the arts. She points to the Riverside Theatre, which almost closed in the 1990s. 'If the city had not stepped in, the theatre would have disappeared,' she says. Today it attracts over 100,000 visitors a year. Economist Daniel Chen takes a different view. He argues that audiences, rather than governments, should decide which art forms survive. Blockbuster films, he notes, make huge profits without any public support. Nevertheless, his own research found that when a museum removed its entry fee, the number of visitors under 25 doubled. Film director Min-ji Park agrees with Moreno but for a different reason. She says that critically acclaimed films rarely make money at the box office. 'My first film had excellent reviews, but if I hadn't received a government grant, I could never have made a second one.' Park also warns that without public funding, only safe, commercial projects would be made.

  1. 1. Government support once saved a building from closing.

  2. 2. People who buy tickets should choose which arts continue.

  3. 3. Free entry attracts more young visitors.

  4. 4. Public money allowed this person to continue working.

  5. 5. Without subsidies, artists would avoid taking risks.

  6. 6. Some forms of entertainment can succeed without help from the state.
