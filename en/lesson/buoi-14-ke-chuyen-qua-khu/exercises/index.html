Bài 1 · Chia động từ

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở quá khứ đơn (động từ bất quy tắc).

  1. 1. I (meet) my wife in 2018.
  2. 2. She (lose) her keys yesterday.
  3. 3. We (go) to the cinema last night.
  4. 4. He (fall) off his bike.
  5. 5. I (hear) a strange noise.
  6. 6. They (leave) home at 7 a.m.
  7. 7. My son (break) a glass this morning.

Bài 2 · Chia động từ

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở quá khứ đơn hoặc quá khứ tiếp diễn.

  1. 1. I (walk) home when it started to rain.
  2. 2. While she was cooking, the phone (ring) .
  3. 3. They (watch) TV when I arrived.
  4. 4. When the accident happened, we (wait) for the bus.
  5. 5. He (see) the accident while he was riding to work.

Bài 3 · Trắc nghiệm

Chọn đáp án đúng.

  1. 1. I didn't ___ you at the party.

  2. 2. ___ I was sleeping, someone knocked on the door.

  3. 3. What ___ yesterday?

  4. 4. They ___ playing football when it started to rain.

  5. 5. ___, nobody was hurt in the accident.

Bài 4 · True / False / Not Given

Đọc câu chuyện rồi chọn T (đúng), F (sai) hoặc NG (không được nhắc đến).

Last Sunday, Tuan and his sister Mai went to the park. While Mai was reading a book, Tuan was playing football with some friends. Suddenly, Tuan fell and hurt his leg. At first, he thought it was nothing, but then he couldn't walk. Mai called their father, and he drove them to the hospital. Luckily, Tuan's leg wasn't broken. The doctor said he should rest for a week.

  1. 1. Tuan was reading a book in the park.

  2. 2. Tuan hurt his leg.

  3. 3. Tuan's leg was broken.

  4. 4. Their mother drove them to the hospital.

  5. 5. The hospital was near the park.

  6. 6. Tuan's friends went to the hospital too.

Bài 5 · Tìm và sửa lỗi

Bài 6 · Biến đổi câu

Viết lại câu theo trật tự còn lại (người + vật ↔ vật + to/for + người).

  1. 1. She gave me a present.
  2. 2. I sent an email to my boss.
  3. 3. He bought his wife a coat.
  4. 4. Can you lend me your calculator?
  5. 5. My uncle told a funny story to us.

Bài 7 · Trắc nghiệm

Chọn đáp án đúng.

  1. 1. My brother ___ a new phone last week.

  2. 2. Please give ___.

  3. 3. She cooked a nice dinner ___ her parents.

  4. 4. Can I borrow your pen? — Sure, I'll ___ it to you.

  5. 5. He ___ us about his trip to Japan.

Bài 8 · Trắc nghiệm

Nghe hội thoại (mỗi người một giọng) rồi chọn đáp án đúng. Cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu, chưa được thì nghe lại.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  1. 1. What was the woman doing when she fell?

  2. 2. Why did she fall?

  3. 3. Who took her to hospital?

Bài 9 · Đúng / Sai (True / False)

Nghe rồi chọn T (đúng) hoặc F (sai). Chú ý chủ thể, con số và các từ như always / never / only.

Nghe bài (thi thật chỉ nghe 1 lần — cố gắng làm sau lần nghe đầu):

  1. 1. The speaker lost the wallet on Sunday.

  2. 2. She was shopping in the market.

  3. 3. The police found the wallet.

  4. 4. A woman brought the wallet back.
